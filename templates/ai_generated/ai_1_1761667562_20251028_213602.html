Welcome to Green Acres Farm!

About Our Farm

At Green Acres Farm, we're dedicated to sustainable farming practices and providing our community with fresh, high-quality produce. Located in the heart of the countryside, our family-owned farm has been cultivating the land for over three generations, upholding traditions while embracing innovative, eco-friendly methods.

Our mission is simple: to grow delicious, nutritious food in harmony with nature. We believe in nurturing the soil, respecting the environment, and ensuring that every fruit, vegetable, and herb we offer is grown with care and passion. From our fields to your table, we strive for transparency, quality, and a commitment to healthy living. Join us in celebrating the bounty of the earth!

Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of produce do you grow?

We grow a wide variety of seasonal fruits and vegetables, including tomatoes, lettuce, kale, carrots, squash, berries, and much more. Our selection changes throughout the year, so check our 'Seasonal Produce' page for current availability!

Are your products organic?

While we are not officially certified organic, we strictly adhere to organic farming principles. This means we use no synthetic pesticides, herbicides, or fertilizers. We focus on natural pest control, crop rotation, and enriching our soil with compost and cover crops.

Can I visit the farm?

Yes, we love visitors! We host open farm days and special events during peak seasons. Please check our 'Events' page or subscribe to our newsletter for dates and details. Private tours can also be arranged upon request.

Do you offer a CSA (Community Supported Agriculture) program?

Absolutely! Our CSA program is a wonderful way to enjoy fresh, weekly harvests directly from our farm. Members receive a box of seasonal produce, often with unique varieties not available elsewhere. Sign-ups typically open in late winter/early spring. Visit our 'CSA Program' page for more information.

Where can I buy your produce?

You can purchase our produce directly from our farm stand during operational hours, through our CSA program, and at select local farmers' markets. Check our 'Find Us' page for a list of market locations and times.
